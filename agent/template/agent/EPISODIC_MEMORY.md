# EPISODIC_MEMORY.md - What Happened
Both sections are written *after* something happened — not upfront as plans or narration. Append-only: add to the end, do not rewrite or delete old entries. The goal is to remember **what was done**, not every low-level step.

"Overview" section: **one short sentence per bullet** capturing what was done. If it needs more than one sentence, split into multiple bullets or drop the less important detail — don't cram.

"Diary" section: one short paragraph per entry — what was done, at a slightly higher level of detail than Overview. **Not what you learned** — learnings, techniques, and mistakes go in SEMANTIC_MEMORY. Skip move-by-move narration and specific coordinates.

Prefix **every bullet and every diary entry** with date and time (e.g. `2026-04-19 16:42`) so ordering is recoverable. Date alone is too coarse.
